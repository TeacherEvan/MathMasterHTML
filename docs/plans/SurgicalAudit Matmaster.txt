  • [P0-4b] Algebraically Broken Equation Generator in DailyChallenge.generateChallenge
      • Coordinates: daily-challenge.js:52-109
      • Root Cause: Out of 11 equation templates in generateChallenge, 6 templates generate mathematically false equations:
          1. "A - X = B" (L54, L79-L80): sets C = A - X, but the template substitutes B (an unrelated random integer) on the right-hand side instead of C.

          5. "A × B ÷ X = C" (L66, L95-L96): computes C = Math.floor((A * B) / X) via integer truncation even when A × B is not divisible by X.
